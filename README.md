# SelfPub Go

**Amazon Ads and KDP royalties in one desktop app.** See the profit of every book, campaign and keyword.

SelfPub Go is a desktop workspace for self-published (KDP) authors and small publishers who advertise on Amazon. Ad reports tell you what you spent, royalty reports tell you what you earned. SelfPub Go puts both together and shows which books actually make money.

Manage Sponsored Products, Brands and Display campaigns, bulk-edit bids and budgets, turn search terms into keywords and negatives, import KDP royalty reports with Kindle Unlimited pages, and track BSR and keyword ranks. Connects through the official Amazon Ads API. Available for macOS and Windows.
